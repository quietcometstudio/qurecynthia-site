The game

Fight monsters, gather and craft, and trade at a market where prices rise and fall through the day.

Towns with their own day

Stalls open in the morning, lamps are lit at dusk, and the townsfolk walk home when it gets late.

Places to explore

Head out through the meadows, Quarry Hills, the Moonlit Wood, the Sunscorch Badlands and Starfall Peaks. Each place has its own monsters, and there are bosses to find.

Two trades each

Every character picks two trades: a fighting class (warrior, archer or mage) and a craft like alchemy, cooking or jewelcrafting. Or two crafts, if you'd rather make things than fight.

A living market

At the Trading Post, prices rise and fall through the day. Place buy and sell orders, read the charts, and listen for rumours that might move the market.

A look around

Playing together

It's a single-player world for now. Online play is planned for later: friends, guilds and a shared Trading Post.
